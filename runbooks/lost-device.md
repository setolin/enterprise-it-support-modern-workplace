# Lost or Stolen Device Runbook

1. Record the report as a high-impact security-related request or incident.
2. Confirm the asset identifier, last known time/location and whether the device may be compromised.
3. Escalate immediately to the Systems Engineer and manager.
4. In a real tenant, initiate remote lock/wipe and revoke sessions according to policy.
5. Disable or quarantine the device identity where supported.
6. Reset or revoke credentials only through the approved identity process.
7. Record actions, times and approvals; do not record secrets.
8. Arrange replacement equipment and user communication.

The lab can document and simulate this workflow. It must not claim that a remote wipe or tenant action occurred unless executed in an authorised test tenant.
