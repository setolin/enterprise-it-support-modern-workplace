# Business Scenario

## Fictional organisation

**Northbridge Clinical Research Ltd (NCR)** is a fictional London-based clinical research services company. It supports research sponsors with study coordination, data operations and regulatory documentation.

## Operating profile

- Approximately 50 staff across London and remote UK locations.
- Hybrid working with Windows 11 laptops as the standard endpoint.
- Microsoft 365 is the target productivity platform.
- Sensitive information includes study documentation, sponsor correspondence and internal HR records.
- A small support function handles first-line triage, endpoint support and escalation to a cloud/infrastructure engineer.

## Departments and example roles

| Department | Example roles | Access pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Executive | Managing Director, Operations Director | Business reporting and controlled company-wide access |
| Clinical Operations | Clinical Research Associate, Study Coordinator | Study workspaces and sponsor collaboration |
| Data & Analytics | Data Analyst, Data Manager | Data platforms and controlled exports |
| Quality & Regulatory | Quality Manager, Regulatory Associate | Quality records and audit evidence |
| Finance & People | Finance Officer, People Coordinator | Finance and HR-restricted resources |
| Commercial | Account Manager, Marketing Executive | CRM, proposals and approved shared resources |
| Technology | IT Support Analyst, Systems Engineer | Privileged support by role and approval |

## Service model

The fictional service desk provides request fulfilment, incident triage, knowledge articles, device support and access administration. High-risk identity or security events escalate to the Systems Engineer; business-critical service interruption escalates to the Operations Director.

## Priority model

Priority is derived from impact and urgency. A multi-user outage or suspected compromise is prioritised above an individual inconvenience, even when the latter is time-sensitive.
